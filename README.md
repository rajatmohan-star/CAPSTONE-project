# AMRG Client Onboarding App

## Project Overview

The **AMRG Client Onboarding App** is an AI-powered solution developed to streamline the initial client assessment process for professional consulting and advisory firms.

The application collects and consolidates client information, business details, financial records, and relevant documents at the beginning of an engagement.

Using AI, the application analyses the submitted information to identify potential **taxation, regulatory, financial, legal, and compliance issues**, generating a structured **Preliminary Client Assessment Report**.

## Key Features

- **Centralised Data Collection:** Collects client information and supporting documents through a structured onboarding process.
- **AI-Powered Analysis:** Reviews submitted information to identify potential issues, risks, and compliance gaps.
- **Automated Issue Identification:** Highlights areas requiring professional attention and further investigation.
- **Report Generation:** Produces a consolidated preliminary assessment report outlining key observations and recommended next steps.
- **Improved Efficiency:** Reduces manual information gathering and enables consultants to focus on high-value advisory services.

## Project Objective

To transform traditional client onboarding into an **AI-driven preliminary diagnostic process**, enabling professional firms to understand client requirements, identify potential risks, and define the scope of advisory services before commencing an engagement.
